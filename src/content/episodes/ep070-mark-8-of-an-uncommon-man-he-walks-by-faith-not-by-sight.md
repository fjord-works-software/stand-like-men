2 Corinthians 5:7 (NKJV) 7 For we walk by faith, not by sight.
An uncommon man does not deny reality, but he refuses to be ruled by it.
He can see facts and still move by faith. He can acknowledge the mountain and still speak to it. 
He can recognize pressure without bowing to it.
He trusts what God has said more than what circumstances are screaming.
